# Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can vote?
Any registered Computer Science student with a completed profile and an
approved account.

### Can I change my vote?
No. Once submitted, a ballot is final. This preserves audit integrity.

### Is my vote secret?
Yes. The audit log records **that** you voted for a position, never
**who** you chose. Only aggregate tallies are visible to admins.

### What is a vote receipt?
A short code returned after you cast a ballot. You can keep it and
match it against published receipt lists to confirm your vote counted.

### Why does my nomination say `pending`?
The electoral committee reviews every nomination manually. Please wait
or contact them.

### Why can't I see any active election?
An election must be in the `active` status and inside its voting window
to appear. Check the **Elections** page for upcoming windows.

### I forgot my password.
Use the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in page.

### Can two people share the same account?
No. Each account is tied to a unique registration number and used to
enforce one-vote-per-person.

### Can admins see who I voted for?
No. Admins can see counts per candidate and that you cast a vote, but
not which candidate you chose.

### Where can I see published results?
On the **Results** page, or `/results/<election-id>`, once the electoral
committee has published them.

### How do I contact the electoral committee?
Use the **Contact** link in the footer.
